# Series Foundation: கண்ணன் கதைகள்

The base description for the whole series: what it's about, where the story is going, who the characters are, and what the world looks like. Read this before writing or generating anything. Episode scripts, prompts and visuals all follow from it.

Related: visual style and prompt blocks → [02-series-bible.md](02-series-bible.md) · Tamil prompts → [02b-character-prompts-tamil.md](02b-character-prompts-tamil.md) · Episode 1 screenplay → [episodes/ep01-screenplay.md](episodes/ep01-screenplay.md)

## 1. The series in one breath

**கண்ணன் கதைகள்** is the life of Lord Krishna told as a warm, cinematic Tamil family drama, episode by episode, from the night he was born in a prison to the day he spoke the Gita on a battlefield.

**Core idea: "இறைவன் குழந்தையாக வந்தால்..." (what if God chose to be a child?)**
The whole universe is inside this boy, yet he wants nothing more than butter, his mother's lap and his friends' laughter. Every episode lives in the gap between those two truths: the *divine* and the *human*. The comedy comes from the human side, the awe from the divine side, and the emotion from the people who love him without fully knowing who he is.

**Promise to the viewer, every episode:**
1. Kannan will make you smile.
2. Someone will love him in a way that moves you.
3. Something will make you say "அடுத்து என்ன?" (what next?).

**Tone:** a family film you can watch with your grandmother and your 6-year-old. Playful, never silly; devotional, never preachy; dramatic, never gory.

**The narrator:** a strong, gripping young man's voice that pulls the audience in and won't let go. He tells the story as if to a friend leaning in to hear what happens next. He speaks only when he adds something: opening a scene, revealing meaning, or setting up the hook. He never explains a joke.

## 2. Where the story is moving: the big arc

The series is told in **five parts (பாகங்கள்)**. Each part has its own world, its own emotional theme and its own villain or challenge. A long episode (10–12 min) covers one or two incidents, and Shorts are cut from each long episode.

| Part | Title | Kannan's age | Setting | Theme | Main threat | Approx. long episodes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **கோகுலம்** (Gokulam) | Newborn to 5 | Mathura prison → Gokulam | **Love is stronger than fear.** Kamsa rules by fear; Gokulam is ruled by love. | Kamsa's demons sent to kill the baby (Putana, Shakata, Trinavarta) | 1–4 |
| 2 | **பிருந்தாவனம்** (Vrindavan) | 5 to 10 | Forests, river, hills, cow pastures | **He protects the ones who love him.** | Forest demons (Vatsa, Baka, Agha), Kaliya, the forest fire, Indra's storm | 5–8 |
| 3 | **புல்லாங்குழல்** (The Flute) | 10 to 16 | Vrindavan in full bloom | **Love and separation.** | No demon; the threat is goodbye. Akrura comes to take him away. | 9–10 |
| 4 | **மதுரா முதல் துவாரகா வரை** | 16 to adult | Mathura, Sandipani's ashram, Dwaraka | **Duty over comfort.** | Kamsa face to face; Jarasandha | 11–15 |
| 5 | **கீதை** (The Gita) | Adult | Hastinapura, Kurukshetra | **Wisdom in the middle of war.** | Adharma itself | 16–20 |

### Threads that run across episodes
| Thread | How it starts | How it develops | Payoff |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Yashoda's love vs. the truth** | Ep 1: she sees the universe in his mouth, then forgets | She keeps getting glimpses (Damodara: the rope that's always two fingers short), keeps choosing to see only her son | Part 3: the farewell. She lets him go to Mathura, and the audience knows what she never knew. |
| **Kamsa's shadow** | Ep 1 hook: his eyes in the lightning, "எங்கேயோ பிறந்துட்டானா?!" | Each demon he sends fails; his fear grows; he's always shown in dark stone, red and black | Part 4: Kamsa vadham |
| **Butter (வெண்ணெய்)** | Ep 1: the funny obsession | A symbol: he steals only from those who love him; butter = the softened heart | Part 3: when he leaves, the butter pots in Gokulam stay full, and that's the saddest image in the series |
| **The flute (புல்லாங்குழல்)** | Ep 1: a small flute tucked in his waist, never played | He starts playing in Part 2; the flute motif grows in the music | Part 3: the flute stops the world. Part 5: he never plays it again |
| **Balarama, the big brother** | Ep 1: the tattletale who won't join the heist | Becomes Kannan's protector and partner against the demons | Part 4: side by side against Kamsa's wrestlers |
| **The friends (Shridama & Subala)** | Ep 1: the comic duo of the butter gang | They grow up with him; they're the audience's eyes, ordinary boys beside God | Part 3: they're left behind in Vrindavan |

### Episode 1's place in the arc
Episode 1 is a **pilot**: it opens in Gokulam when Kannan is about 5 so new viewers first meet him at his most lovable. Its last scene flashes back to the stormy night of his birth, and **Episodes 2–3 tell the birth and the infant demons**. From Episode 4 the series moves strictly forward in time.

> Tradition note: in the Bhagavatam, the butter theft and mud-eating happen while Krishna is a toddler (around 2–3). We show him at about 5 so his expressions and dialogue work on screen. This is a deliberate adaptation; say so if viewers ask.

## 3. The characters

### 3.1 கண்ணன் (Kannan / Child Krishna), the heart of the series

**Role:** hero. The Supreme Lord, living as a cowherd's son.
**Age in Part 1:** about 5.

**Personality**
- **Mischievous genius:** he plans heists like a little general. Every problem is a game, and he always has "ஒரு வழி".
- **Generous:** he steals butter, but shares it all with his friends, the monkeys, everyone. He never eats alone.
- **Master of the innocent face:** when caught, he lies sweetly and outrageously ("குரங்கு மாமா பூசிட்டுப் போயிட்டாரு!"). Everyone knows he's lying, and nobody can stay angry.
- **Hidden depth:** for a split second, in rare moments (the knowing smile before the universe reveal), the audience sees something ancient and calm behind the child's eyes. **Use this sparingly**, once per episode at most. It's the series' most powerful image.
- **Never cruel, never humiliated:** his pranks never hurt anyone, and he is never made to look foolish or pathetic.

**How he talks:** short, quick, playful sentences in everyday colloquial Tamil. He uses "டேய்" with friends, "அம்மா" with sweetness, and "அக்கா" and "மாமா" with neighbours. He giggles mid-sentence. When he's being "innocent", his voice goes sing-song. In the rare divine moments, his voice becomes calm and slow, with no giggle at all.

**Relationships**
- **Yashoda:** his whole world. He tests her patience all day and falls asleep in her lap every night.
- **Balarama:** big brother; Kannan teases him but listens to him.
- **Shridama & Subala:** his gang; he's the leader.
- **The gopis:** his "victims", who secretly wait for him to come and steal.
- **Kamsa:** an enemy he hasn't met yet.

**Arc in Part 1:** from a mischievous child → glimpses of divinity (universe in his mouth) → protector (the demons fail against him).

**Appearance (detailed)**
- **Skin:** deep blue-black, the colour of a monsoon rain cloud (கார்முகில் வண்ணன்), with a soft natural sheen. Never teal, never purple, never bright cartoon blue.
- **Face:** round, soft cheeks; small button nose; full small lips usually curled in a half-smile showing tiny teeth; a small black dhrishti dot on his **left** cheek.
- **Eyes:** large, almond-shaped, dark brown, lined with black kohl; long eyelashes. Eyes are the most expressive part of him: mischief, innocence and, rarely, ancient calm.
- **Hair:** black curls tied in a small topknot on top of the head; a few curls fall on his forehead. **One peacock feather** stands in the topknot, held by a thin, simple gold crown-band.
- **Clothing:** a bright turmeric-yellow silk dhoti (பீதாம்பரம்) tied at the waist with a gold sash; bare chest.
- **Jewellery:** a thin gold waist-chain with tiny bells (அரைஞாண்); gold armlets; small gold earrings; a tulsi-bead necklace; a short garland of white jasmine and orange marigold; gold anklets (கொலுசு) with tiny bells, so he jingles when he moves, which often gets him caught. Barefoot.
- **Props:** a small bamboo flute tucked into his waist sash (not played in Part 1); frequently butter on his face, hands or nose.
- **Body language:** always moving: bouncing, tiptoeing, climbing, running with his elbows out.

**Signature gestures:** a finger to his lips with "ஷ்ஷ்"; a slow wink to camera; hiding his hands behind his back when caught; looking up with enormous eyes; holding his anklets so they don't jingle.

**Voice:** high, bright, giggly, cheeky; a neutral Chennai Tamil accent.

**Rules:** he is always the most beautiful thing in the frame. He is never shown in pain or fear in a way that is upsetting. He is the hero of every scene, even when he's off-screen.

### 3.2 யசோதை (Yashoda), the mother

**Role:** Kannan's foster mother, Nandagopar's wife, the emotional centre of Part 1.
**Age:** about 28.

**Personality**
- **All love, pretending to be strict:** she scolds with her mouth and forgives with her eyes. Her "anger" never lasts more than a sentence before it cracks into a smile.
- **Busy and capable:** she runs a large cowherd household: churning, milking, cooking, managing the women's gossip at her door.
- **Fiercely protective:** the moment Kannan might be hurt (eating mud!), the play stops and real worry takes over.
- **Faith without knowing:** she glimpses his divinity and chooses, again and again, to see only her child. This is her greatness, not her ignorance.

**How she talks:** warm, everyday Tamil from a village mother. She calls him "கண்ணா" (dozens of ways: warning, loving, worried). She says "என் செல்லம்" and "என் செல்லத் திருடா", and her scolding comes with a smile ("இப்போ பாரு...!"). She sings lullabies (ஆராரோ ஆரிரரோ).

**Relationships:** Kannan (life itself); Balarama (loves him as her own; he's Rohini's son); Nandagopar (affectionate partnership); the gopis (friends and gossip partners, who are also his "victims").

**Arc in Part 1:** amused mother → frightened mother (the demons) → the mother who saw the universe and chose love.

**Appearance (detailed)**
- **Skin:** warm wheat-brown.
- **Face:** kind oval face; large expressive eyes that crease when she laughs; a red kumkum dot on her forehead; a small nose stud on the left.
- **Hair:** long, thick black hair in a single braid down her back, decorated with a string of jasmine. Loose and slightly untidy while churning in the morning.
- **Clothing:** a cotton saree in **deep red with a mustard-yellow border**, draped in a simple ancient style (no modern pleats), with a short-sleeved blouse in a matching red. The end of the saree (முந்தானை) is often used to wipe Kannan's face.
- **Jewellery:** glass and gold bangles on both wrists (they clink when she churns and when she hugs); a simple gold necklace; silver anklets (கொலுசு) whose sound warns the boys she's coming.
- **Body language:** hands on hips when "angry"; kneeling to Kannan's level when she talks to him seriously; scooping him into her lap.

**Signature gestures:** a raised eyebrow; wagging a finger; wiping his face with her saree; a forehead kiss; the long, sing-song "கண்ணா...!" warning.

**Voice:** warm alto; scolding that dissolves into laughter.

**Rules:** never shown hitting him. Even in the Damodara episode, where she ties him to the mortar, it is out of love and done gently.

### 3.3 நந்தகோபர் (Nandagopar), the father
Appears mostly in the background in Episode 1 (leaving with the herdsmen at dawn; mentioned as "அப்பா"). He becomes more important from Episode 2.

- **Role:** chief of the cowherds of Gokulam; Kannan's foster father.
- **Age:** about 35.
- **Personality:** gentle, dignified, generous, a little absent-minded; laughs from his belly.
- **Talk:** few words, calm and fatherly: "டேய் கண்ணா, அம்மா சொல்றதைக் கேளு."
- **Appearance:** tall and broad; warm brown skin; a full black beard with streaks of grey; a white turban with a saffron band; a white dhoti and a saffron shoulder cloth; a gold earring in each ear; a tall wooden herding staff. Kind eyes.
- **Voice:** deep, slow, warm.

---

### 3.4 பலராமன் (Balarama), the big brother
- **Role:** Kannan's elder brother (son of Rohini, raised in the same house).
- **Age:** about 7.
- **Personality:** proud, responsible, rule-following, strong, a bit bossy, quick to tattle, but he'd fight a demon for his brother. He loves Kannan more than he admits.
- **Talk:** a big-brother tone: "டேய்", "நான் அம்மா கிட்ட சொல்லிடுவேன் பாரு!", "நானே பார்த்தேன்!"
- **Arc:** tattletale (Ep 1) → protector (Part 2) → partner (Part 4).
- **Appearance:** fair, light skin (the traditional contrast to Kannan's dark skin); sturdy and strong for his age; short wavy black hair in a topknot; a deep indigo-blue cotton dhoti; simple gold earrings and armlets; a small white conch-bead necklace. In later parts he carries a small plough-shaped toy and, as an adult, a plough.
- **Gestures:** arms crossed; pointing accusingly.
- **Voice:** a slightly older boy's voice; firm.

### 3.5 ஸ்ரீதாமா (Shridama), the strong one in the gang
- **Role:** Kannan's best friend, the "muscle" of the butter gang.
- **Age:** about 6.
- **Personality:** big, cheerful, always hungry, loyal to the death, laughs loudly; he's the bottom of every human tower and complains about it every time.
- **Talk:** loud and simple: "வந்துட்டான்டா நம்ம தலைவன்!", "கண்ணா... நீ குட்டியா இருந்தாலும் கனமா இருக்கடா!"
- **Appearance:** medium brown skin; chubby and sturdy; a round face with big cheeks; short messy hair held by a green cloth headband; a short off-white cotton dhoti; a small wooden cowbell on a string around his neck (it clonks when he runs).
- **Gestures:** licking his lips at butter; rubbing his shoulders after the tower; a belly laugh.
- **Voice:** a chubby kid's voice; loud, laughing.

---

### 3.6 சுபலன் (Subala), the worrier in the gang
- **Role:** the gang's nervous voice of caution, which no one ever listens to.
- **Age:** about 5.
- **Personality:** small, anxious, sensible, always predicts doom ("மாட்டிக்கப் போறோம்!"), always right, always comes along anyway. He's the funniest reaction face in the show.
- **Talk:** whiny and worried: "ஐயோ!", "டேய்...", "எப்படிடா எட்டும்?", "ஆனா எனக்குப் பசிக்குது!"
- **Appearance:** light brown skin; thin and small; big worried eyes with eyebrows permanently raised; hair tied in a tiny tuft on top; a short saffron-orange cotton dhoti; a red bead string on his wrist; a small shepherd's stick that always ends up used in the heist.
- **Gestures:** biting his nails; covering his eyes; peeking through his fingers.
- **Voice:** small and nervous.

### 3.7 கோமதி அக்கா (Gomathi Akka), the neighbour *(invented character)*
- **Role:** Kannan's favourite "victim" and the loudest complainer among the gopis.
- **Age:** about 30.
- **Personality:** sharp-tongued, quick-tempered, dramatic, and secretly Kannan's biggest fan. She complains about him all day and would be heartbroken if he stopped stealing from her.
- **Talk:** fast, loud village Tamil with a theatrical flair: "அடேய் கண்ணா!! திரும்பவும் நீயா?!", "திருடனா இருந்தாலும்... இவ்வளவு அழகான திருடனை என்ன பண்றது?"
- **Appearance:** dusky skin; a sharp but kind face; large expressive eyes; hair in a bun with a few jasmine flowers; a parrot-green cotton saree with a maroon border; brass bangles; a nose ring; usually carries a brass water pot or a ladle (her weapon).
- **Note:** recurring character for Part 1; a fictional addition, not in the scriptures.

### 3.8 The other gopis *(invented names and looks)*
- **பாட்டி (Paati):** about 60, an old grandmother. Silver hair in a bun, a white saree with a thin red border, a wrinkled smiling face, a churning stick in her hand. She complains slowly and with dignity ("பால் கறக்குற நேரத்துக்கு முன்னாடியே...").
- **கமலா (Kamala):** about 22, young and lively. A mustard-yellow saree with a green border, flowers in her braid, a brass pot on her hip. Dramatic gestures; the first to burst out laughing.

### 3.9 குரங்கு மாமா (the monkey), a recurring comic animal
- A grey langur with a black face and a long tail; cheeky; always at the window when there's butter. Kannan calls him "குரங்கு மாமா". He gets blamed for everything. Reuse him across Part 1 episodes as a running joke.

### 3.10 கதைசொல்லி (the Narrator)
- Never seen. A strong young man's voice (late twenties): deep, confident and gripping, with controlled intensity and dramatic pauses before the last word ("...வெண்ணெய்!"). He speaks to the audience as "நீங்க" and sometimes asks the hook question at the end ("யசோதா என்ன பார்த்தாங்க?").

### 3.11 The shadows of Mathura (Episode 1 glimpses only, full designs in Ep 2)
- **கம்சன் (Kamsa):** king of Mathura, Devaki's brother. Huge and broad-shouldered; a heavy jagged gold crown; a dark red cloak over black armour; cruel, sleepless, glinting eyes. In Episode 1 we see **only his eyes in lightning** and hear one line. He is driven by fear, not strength, so play it that way. Never cartoonish.
- **வசுதேவர் & தேவகி (Vasudeva & Devaki):** Kannan's birth parents, imprisoned by Kamsa. Torn royal clothes; iron chains; a dignity that suffering hasn't broken. Silhouettes only in Episode 1.

## 4. The world: backgrounds and locations

### 4.1 Design principles
- **Era:** Dwapara Yuga, ancient India. No metal tools beyond simple iron and brass; no paper, glass windows, modern furniture or temples with tall gopurams.
- **Region:** Gokulam and Vrindavan sit on the banks of the Yamuna in the Braj region (north India). The geography is northern: flat green pastures, sandy riverbanks, kadamba and banyan trees, peacocks.
- **Deliberate Tamil touch:** to feel close to a Tamil audience, everyday details are familiar to Tamil homes: **kolam** at the door, **jasmine** in the hair, **kolusu**, the **thinnai** veranda, brass vessels, **ஆராரோ** lullabies. This blend is a stylistic choice of the series; keep it consistent.
- **Gokulam = warmth and life:** earth colours, gold light, animals everywhere, things always slightly messy and lived-in.
- **Mathura = fear and stone:** dark grey stone, red and black, torchlight, rain and lightning. Never show Mathura in warm sunlight in Part 1.
- **Divine moments = light from within:** light comes *from* Kannan (gold, white, indigo), not from the sky.

### 4.2 Gokulam village (overview)
A prosperous cowherd settlement on the bank of the Yamuna. About 40–50 homes grouped around Nandagopar's large house in the centre, with dusty lanes between them. Each home has a cowshed. Fields and pastures surround the village. There's a large banyan tree with a stone platform where children play, and a path down to the river ghat where women fill water pots. The air is always full of sounds: cowbells, birds, churning, children, women calling to each other.

**Time-of-day looks:**
| Time | Light | Colours | Life in the village |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dawn | Soft pink-gold, river mist, long shadows | Pink, gold, pale blue | Churning, milking, men leaving with the herds |
| Morning | Bright warm sun, dust motes in beams | Butter-yellow, terracotta, green | Children playing, women at the river |
| Afternoon | Golden, hazy | Gold, olive green | Play by the river, cattle resting |
| Dusk | Orange sky, first oil lamps | Orange, deep brown, lamp gold | Herds returning in clouds of dust (கோதூளி), lamps lit |
| Night | Moonlight and oil lamps | Indigo, silver, warm lamp-orange | Lullabies, crickets, quiet |

**H. The cosmic vision (Episode 1 only)**
Starfields, spiral galaxies, the sun, moon and planets, mountains, oceans, the elements, and finally Gokulam itself, all in **gold, white and deep indigo**, reverent and vast, never scary. See Scene 8 in the screenplay.

### 4.5 The sound world
- **Gokulam:** cowbells, mooing, birdsong (koels, sparrows, peacock calls), the swish of the churning rope, bangles, kolusu, children laughing, women calling across lanes.
- **Yamuna:** flowing water, wind in tall grass, distant flute.
- **Mathura:** thunder, rain, chains, heavy footsteps on stone, a low drum.
- **Divine:** a low humming "om", temple-bell shimmer, wind chimes, rising strings.
- **Series motif:** a four-note bansuri phrase at every opening and ending.
