## The story of this episode in one paragraph
It's an ordinary morning in Gokulam, and little Kannan is up to his usual tricks. He leads his friends Shridama and Subala in a daring butter raid on Gomathi Akka's storeroom, escapes, and fools the complaining gopis by pretending to have slept in his mother's lap all morning. Then he raids his own mother's pot and talks his way out with the most outrageous excuse in Gokulam. But in the afternoon, by the Yamuna, he quietly eats a pinch of mud. Balarama tells on him. When Yashoda orders him to open his mouth, she sees not mud but the entire universe, including herself. Kannan gently lets her forget so she can go on loving him as just her little boy. That night, as she sings him to sleep, she whispers: "Who are you, Kannan?" And far away, in a storm over Mathura, a king's eyes burn in the lightning…

## COLD OPEN

### INT. கோமதி அக்கா வீடு – இருட்டு அறை (GOMATHI'S STOREROOM) – MORNING
**Background:** a pitch-dark windowless storeroom. Dozens of clay pots hang from rope slings at different heights. A thin beam of light comes from one tiny high window. Dust floats in the beam.

**Story beat:** we drop the audience straight into the action with a mysterious glowing child, before we explain anything.

Darkness. Then a soft blue-gold glow appears, coming from a **small child**. We see only his kohl-lined eyes and a peacock feather peeking over the rim of a clay pot full of white butter. His small blue hand is deep in the butter.

He lifts a butter-covered finger to his lips and looks straight at us.

**கண்ணன்** *(whispering, cheeky)*
ஷ்ஷ்... யார்கிட்டயும் சொல்லாதீங்க!

*(He giggles.)*

Down the corridor, the flame of an oil lamp approaches, flickering on the walls.

**கோமதி** *(off-screen, suspicious)*
யாரது? யாரு உள்ள?

Kannan grins, winks at us, and is gone. The pot is left swinging on its rope.

### EXT. கோகுலம் (GOKULAM VILLAGE) – DAWN
**Background:** an aerial view of the cowherd village on the Yamuna at sunrise: thatched mud houses, kolams at the doorsteps, cows with painted horns, cooking smoke, mist on the river, a pink-gold sky.

**கதைசொல்லி** *(engaging young storyteller, a hint of a smile)*
இவன் தான் கண்ணன். கோகுலத்துல எல்லாரும் இவனை உயிரா நேசிக்கிறாங்க...

A village lane: Kannan runs laughing with a lump of butter in his hand. Three gopis chase him, waving ladles. Cows scatter.

**கதைசொல்லி** *(pause, then amused)*
...ஆனா எல்லார் வீட்டு வெண்ணெயும் இவனைக் கண்டாலே பயப்படுது!

**TITLE** *(a peacock feather sweeps across the screen; the flute motif plays)*
**கண்ணன் கதைகள்**
**அத்தியாயம் 1: கோகுலத்தின் குட்டித் திருடன்**

## SCENE 1: விடியல் (DAWN, THE BUTTER IS CHURNED)

### EXT./INT. நந்தகோபர் வீடு – முற்றம் (NANDA'S HOUSE – COURTYARD) – DAWN
**Background:** the courtyard of the cowherd chief's house: a tulsi plant on its little raised platform in the centre, cows in the shed along one side, wooden pillars with a thatched roof around the edges, brass pots, a fresh kolam at the gate. Soft dawn light and cool mist.

**Story beat:** show the home, the morning ritual, and the love between mother and son, and set up the "problem": the butter pot is put out of reach.

Herdsmen led by **Nandagopar** (tall, bearded, white turban) leave through the gate with their staffs, driving the cows out to pasture. Cowbells fade.

**கதைசொல்லி**
கோகுலத்துல காலை விடியறதே... தயிர் கடையற சத்தத்துல தான்.

**Yashoda** sits beside a huge clay pot, pulling the rope of the churning rod: swish, swish, swish. Her bangles clink in rhythm. Her hair is still loose from sleep. Eyes half-closed, she hums.

**யசோதை** *(softly singing to herself)*
ஆராரோ... ஆரிரரோ... என் கண்ணே... கார்முகில் வண்ணா...

Close on the pot: fresh white butter rises and gathers on the swirling buttermilk.

### INT. யசோதையின் வீடு – அறை (THE MAIN ROOM) – CONTINUOUS
**Background:** lime-washed mud walls, a reed mat on the floor, brass vessels on shelves, the empty rope sling (உறி) hanging from the beam above. Morning light through the window.

**Kannan** is asleep on a reed mat, hugging a small wooden cow toy. His nose twitches. He sniffs. One eye pops open.

**கண்ணன்** *(sleepy murmur)*
ம்ம்ம்... வெண்ணெய் வாசனை...

He tiptoes across the floor. *Jingle, jingle* go his anklets. He freezes, looks at us guiltily, then bends down and holds both anklets in his hands to silence them, waddling forward ridiculously.

### EXT. முற்றம் (COURTYARD) – CONTINUOUS

Kannan wraps his arms around Yashoda from behind and lays his cheek on her back. She keeps churning, smiling without turning around.

**கண்ணன்** *(sweet, pitiful)*
அம்மா... எனக்குப் பசிக்குது...

**யசோதை** *(teasing, she knows him too well)*
பசிக்குதா? இல்ல வெண்ணெய் வேணுமா?

Kannan comes around to face her and holds out both hands, pouting.

**கண்ணன்**
ரெண்டும் தான்!

Yashoda laughs, scoops a small ball of butter and places it in his palms.

**யசோதை**
இந்தா, கொஞ்சம் தான். மீதி அப்பா வந்ததும்.

Kannan swallows the whole thing in one gulp and immediately holds his hand out again with huge, shining eyes.

**கண்ணன்**
இன்னும் கொஞ்சம்?

**யசோதை** *(firm, shaking her head)*
ம்ஹூம்!

### INT. அறை (MAIN ROOM) – MOMENTS LATER

Yashoda stands on her toes and places the butter pot into the rope sling (உறி) hanging high from the ceiling beam. She ties the knot, dusts off her hands, and gives Kannan a triumphant look.

**யசோதை** *(playfully challenging)*
இப்போ பாரு... இதை எப்படி எடுக்குறேன்னு நானும் பாக்குறேன்!

She leaves. Kannan stands alone, looking up at the pot far above him. Slowly, he turns to us. A sly smile. His eyebrows wiggle.

**கதைசொல்லி**
யசோதா அம்மா நினைச்சாங்க... பிரச்சனை முடிஞ்சதுன்னு. ஆனா கண்ணனுக்கு... இது ஆரம்பம் தான்!

## SCENE 2: திட்டம் (THE GANG AND THE PLAN)

### EXT. கிராமத் தெரு / ஆலமரம் (VILLAGE LANE & THE BANYAN TREE) – MORNING
**Background:** a dusty lane between thatched houses with kolams at every door, a bullock cart, wandering cows. At the end is a huge banyan tree with hanging roots and a round stone platform, the gang's headquarters. Bright sun and dappled shade.

**Story beat:** introduce the friends and Balarama; set up the heist; Balarama's refusal and threat ("I'll tell Amma") is planted here and pays off in Scene 7.

Kannan runs down the lane, his peacock feather bouncing, dodging a cow and a bullock cart.

Under the banyan tree, **Shridama** (chubby, green headband) and **Subala** (small, worried eyes, saffron dhoti) are playing marbles. Shridama looks up and beams.

**ஸ்ரீதாமா** *(loud, delighted)*
வந்துட்டான்டா நம்ம தலைவன்!

Kannan crouches between them and throws his arms around both their shoulders, like a general briefing his army.

**கண்ணன்** *(excited whisper)*
டேய், ஒரு பெரிய வேலை இருக்கு. கோமதி அக்கா வீட்டுல இன்னைக்கு புது வெண்ணெய் கடைஞ்சிருக்காங்க!

Shridama licks his lips. Subala's eyes go wide with horror.

**சுபலன்** *(whiny, scared)*
ஐயோ! போன வாரம் மாட்டினப்போ அவங்க தொரத்துனது மறந்துட்டியா?

Kannan picks up a stick and draws in the dust: a house, a pot, a mortar, a window. He taps the drawing proudly.

**கண்ணன்**
அதுக்குத்தான் இந்த தடவை ஒரு திட்டம்!

A shadow falls over the drawing. The boys turn. **Balarama** stands behind them, arms crossed, one eyebrow raised.

**பலராமன்** *(suspicious big brother)*
என்ன திட்டம்? திருட்டுத் திட்டமா?

Kannan switches instantly to his sweetest face.

**கண்ணன்** *(sugar-sweet)*
அண்ணா, நீயும் வாயேன்! உனக்குத்தான் பெரிய பங்கு!

**பலராமன்** *(turning his face away, firm)*
நான் வரல. அம்மா கிட்ட சொல்லிடுவேன் பாரு!

Balarama walks off. Kannan shrugs at his friends.

**கண்ணன்** *(waving it off)*
விடுடா, அண்ணன் எப்பவுமே இப்படித்தான். வாங்க!

The three boys sprint off down the lane in a cloud of dust, laughing.

## SCENE 3: கோமதி அக்கா வீடு (THE HEIST)

### EXT. கோமதி அக்கா வீடு (GOMATHI'S HOUSE) – MORNING
**Background:** a smaller thatched house with a hay pile beside it and a green-painted doorframe. Bright, quiet lane.

**Story beat:** the fun set-piece, which also delivers the first sign that Kannan is no ordinary child: his body lights up the dark room (from the Bhagavatam). It also pays off the cold open.

**Gomathi** (green saree, brass pot on her hip) steps out of her door.

**கோமதி** *(to herself, walking off)*
யமுனைக்குப் போய் தண்ணி எடுத்துட்டு வந்துடறேன்...

Behind the hay pile, three small heads rise one above the other.

**கண்ணன்** *(whisper)*
போயிட்டாங்க... வாங்க!

### INT. இருட்டு அறை (THE DARK STOREROOM) – CONTINUOUS
**Background:** the same storeroom from the cold open: pitch dark, pots hanging everywhere, one tiny window.

The boys shuffle in and bump into each other in total darkness.

**சுபலன்** *(scared whisper)*
டேய்... ஒண்ணுமே தெரியலடா...

Kannan steps forward. A gentle **blue-gold light** begins to glow from his skin and jewellery, spreading softly until the whole room is lit. Dozens of butter pots hang above them. The friends' faces fill with wonder.

**ஸ்ரீதாமா** *(awed)*
ஆஹா... கண்ணா, நீ இருந்தா விளக்கே வேண்டாம்டா!

**கண்ணன்** *(pleased, giggling)*
ஹிஹி!

Kannan points at a heavy stone mortar in the corner like a commander.

**கண்ணன்**
அந்த உரலை இங்க உருட்டுங்கடா!

Shridama and Subala strain and push and grunt, rolling the mortar under the pots, faces red.

The tower goes up: Subala holds the mortar steady, Shridama stands on top of it, and Kannan climbs onto Shridama's shoulders. Shridama's legs wobble.

**ஸ்ரீதாமா** *(straining)*
கண்ணா... நீ குட்டியா இருந்தாலும் கனமா இருக்கடா!

The pot is still just out of reach. Kannan grabs Subala's shepherd's stick and pokes a small hole in the bottom of the pot. A stream of curd and butter pours down, straight into Subala's open mouth below.

**சுபலன்** *(gulping, blissful)*
ம்ம்ம்... ஆஹா... அமிர்தம்!

At the tiny high window, three monkeys appear, chattering. Kannan tosses them lumps of butter.

**கண்ணன்** *(generous)*
குரங்கு மாமா, நீங்களும் சாப்பிடுங்க!

### EXT. கோமதி அக்கா வீடு – CONTINUOUS

Gomathi returns with a full water pot. She stops. Chattering, giggling and slurping come from inside. Her eyes narrow. She sets the pot down.

**கோமதி**
உள்ள என்ன சத்தம்?!

### INT. இருட்டு அறை – CONTINUOUS

The door bursts open. Daylight floods in. Everyone freezes: three boys in a tower, three monkeys with full cheeks, butter dripping everywhere.

**கோமதி** *(furious, theatrical)*
அடேய் கண்ணா!! திரும்பவும் நீயா?!

Chaos. The tower collapses. Shridama and Subala dive out of the window behind the monkeys. Kannan lands on his bottom, looks up at Gomathi, grins, and darts right between her feet and out the door.

**கண்ணன்** *(over his shoulder, running)*
வெண்ணெய் ரொம்ப நல்லா இருந்துச்சு அக்கா!

### EXT. கிராமத் தெரு (VILLAGE LANE) – CONTINUOUS

Gomathi charges down the lane waving a ladle. Other women come to their doors to see what's happening.

**கோமதி** *(shouting)*
நில்லுடா! இன்னைக்கு உங்க அம்மா கிட்ட சொல்லாம விடமாட்டேன்!

## SCENE 4: புகார் (THE GOPIS' COMPLAINT)

### EXT. யசோதையின் வீடு – திண்ணை (YASHODA'S VERANDA) – LATE MORNING
**Background:** the raised thinnai along the front of the house, two wooden pillars with a jasmine creeper, a basket of jasmine. Bright late-morning light. The gate and lane are visible.

**Story beat:** comedy and affection. The whole village "suffers" from Kannan, and the whole village adores him. Yashoda can't bring herself to punish him (from the Bhagavatam). The gopis' complaints are really love.

Yashoda sits on the thinnai stringing jasmine. Kannan races through the gate, panting, butter on his lip, jumps into her lap, curls up and squeezes his eyes shut.

**யசோதை** *(puzzled)*
என்னடா, ஓடி வந்து தூங்குற?

**கண்ணன்** *(eyes still shut, very serious)*
ஷ்ஷ்... நான் காலையிலிருந்து இங்கதான் தூங்கிட்டு இருக்கேன்.

The gate creaks. Four women march in: **Gomathi** in front (butter splashed on her saree), **Paati** with her churning stick, young **Kamala** with a pot on her hip, and one more gopi.

**கோமதி** *(hands on hips)*
யசோதா! உன் பையனைக் கொஞ்சம் பாரு!

**பாட்டி** *(wagging a finger, slow and dignified)*
பால் கறக்குற நேரத்துக்கு முன்னாடியே கன்னுக்குட்டியை அவுத்து விட்டுடறான்!

**கமலா** *(dramatic gestures)*
வெண்ணெயை குரங்குக்குக் குடுக்கறான்! குரங்கு சாப்பிடலைன்னா பானையையே உடைச்சிடறான்!

**கோமதி** *(pointing at the "sleeping" child)*
பிடிச்சா சிரிச்சுக்கிட்டே ஓடிடறான்! இன்னைக்கு கூட எங்க வீட்டுல...

Yashoda looks down at the angelic sleeping face in her lap, then up at the women, amused.

**யசோதை** *(raising her eyebrows)*
இவனா? இவன் சொல்றான், காலையிலிருந்து இங்கதான் தூங்குறானாம்!

Close on Kannan: **one eye slowly opens.** He winks at the gopis. There's butter on his lip.

The women gasp, then burst out laughing.

**கமலா** *(pointing, laughing)*
பாரு பாரு! உதட்டுல இன்னும் வெண்ணெய் இருக்கு!

Gomathi bends down and pinches Kannan's cheek, laughing despite herself.

**கோமதி** *(melting)*
திருடனா இருந்தாலும்... இவ்வளவு அழகான திருடனை என்ன பண்றது?

The women leave through the gate, still laughing, each one looking back at Kannan one last time.

**கதைசொல்லி**
கோகுலத்துப் பெண்கள் புகார் சொல்லத்தான் வந்தாங்க... ஆனா உண்மையில, கண்ணனைப் பார்க்கத்தான் வந்தாங்க.

## SCENE 5: வீட்டு வெண்ணெய் (THE HOME POT)

### INT. யசோதையின் வீடு – அறை (MAIN ROOM) – MIDDAY
**Background:** the main room again, with the butter pot hanging high in the உறி where Yashoda put it this morning, the mortar in the corner, and the window with the neem tree.

**Story beat:** the payoff of Scene 1. Yashoda's "impossible" hiding place falls in minutes. It's also the sweetest mother–son moment of the episode.

Yashoda stands at the door with a milk pail and wags a finger at Kannan, who sits on his mat looking like an angel.

**யசோதை**
கண்ணா, நான் மாட்டுக் கொட்டகைக்குப் போயிட்டு வரேன். வெண்ணெய் பக்கம் போகக் கூடாது, சரியா?

**கண்ணன்** *(perfectly sweet)*
சரிம்மா!

The moment she's gone, Kannan whistles. Shridama's and Subala's heads pop up at the window.

The tower again: the mortar is rolled under the sling, Shridama stands on it, and Kannan climbs onto his shoulders.

**ஸ்ரீதாமா** *(straining)*
சீக்கிரம் கண்ணா... தோள் வலிக்குது!

**கண்ணன்** *(stretching, then triumphant)*
அசையாதடா... இதோ... கிடைச்சுடுச்சு!

His hand sinks into the pot. He tosses butter down to his friends, who catch it in their mouths, and to the monkey who has appeared at the window.

**கண்ணன்** *(laughing)*
எல்லாருக்கும் உண்டு! குரங்கு மாமா, உனக்கும் தான்!

*Jingle... jingle...* Silver anklets on the threshold. The boys freeze.

**சுபலன்** *(terrified whisper)*
டேய்... கொலுசு சத்தம்... அம்மா!

**யசோதை** *(off-screen, long and sing-song)*
கண்ணா...!

The tower collapses. The pot falls and smashes. The friends vanish through the window. Kannan sits alone on the floor among the broken pieces, butter all over his face, and hides both hands behind his back.

Yashoda stands in the doorway, hands on hips, trying very hard to look angry.

**யசோதை** *(stern, but her voice cracks with amusement)*
கண்ணா, திரும்பவும் வெண்ணெய் திருடித் தின்னியா?

**கண்ணன்** *(huge innocent eyes, sing-song)*
அம்மா, நான் ஒண்ணுமே பண்ணலையே!

Yashoda kneels down to his level, wipes a dab of butter off his nose with one finger, and holds it up in front of his eyes.

**யசோதை** *(teasing)*
அப்போ உன் மூக்குல இருக்கிறது என்ன?

Kannan looks at the finger, cross-eyed. He thinks. Then he brightens and points confidently at the window.

**கண்ணன்** *(totally confident)*
அதுவா... குரங்கு மாமா பூசிட்டுப் போயிட்டாரும்மா!

Outside the window, the monkey chatters as if in protest.

Yashoda can't hold it any longer. She bursts out laughing, pulls him into a tight hug, and wipes his face with the end of her saree.

**யசோதை** *(laughing, adoring)*
என் செல்லத் திருடா...

---

## SCENE 6: யமுனைக் கரை (THE RIVERBANK)

### EXT. யமுனை நதிக்கரை (YAMUNA RIVERBANK) – AFTERNOON
**Background:** a golden afternoon. Sandy and wet clay banks, shining blue-green water, kadamba trees with round orange flowers, tall grass, grazing calves, a peacock. Far across the river, the faint walls of a distant fortress, Mathura, which no one in the scene notices.

**Story beat:** the turning point. A playful scene turns mysterious when Kannan eats the mud, and Balarama's promise from Scene 2 ("I'll tell Amma") comes true.

**கதைசொல்லி**
சாயங்காலம். யமுனைக் கரையில பசங்க விளையாட்டு.

The boys play tag on the bank. Kannan runs ahead, looking back over his shoulder.

**கண்ணன்** *(laughing, teasing)*
டேய், என்னைப் பிடிக்க முடியுமா? பிடிச்சுப் பாரு!

**ஸ்ரீதாமா** *(chasing, puffing)*
இருடா கண்ணா! இன்னைக்கு உன்னை விடமாட்டேன்!

Kannan hides behind a kadamba tree, giggling. Then he squats by the wet clay at the water's edge.

Close on his small blue fingers as he picks up a pinch of wet earth. He looks at it curiously, the way you'd look at something you've known forever. He puts it in his mouth and smiles serenely. For a heartbeat, a faint golden sparkle passes across his eyes. *(One single bell note.)*

Further along the bank, Balarama stops dead, eyes wide.

**பலராமன்** *(shouting, pointing)*
டேய்! கண்ணன் மண்ணு திங்கிறான்!

Shridama and Subala run up to him and gasp.

**பலராமன்** *(determined)*
வாங்கடா, அம்மா கிட்ட சொல்லுவோம்!

They race off toward the village.

---

## SCENE 7: "என் வாயைப் பாரு" ("LOOK IN MY MOUTH")

### EXT. நந்தகோபர் வீடு – முற்றம் (COURTYARD) – DUSK
**Background:** the courtyard at dusk: an orange sky, the first clay oil lamps being lit, herds returning outside in clouds of golden dust (கோதூளி). Warm lamp glow.

**Story beat:** the confrontation. For the first time in the episode, Kannan isn't playful: he's strangely calm. The audience senses that something big is coming.

Yashoda is lighting the evening lamps. The three boys burst into the courtyard, all shouting at once.

**பலராமன்**
அம்மா! அம்மா! கண்ணன் மண்ணு தின்னான்! நானே பார்த்தேன்!

**ஸ்ரீதாமா & சுபலன்**
ஆமா அம்மா! நாங்களும் பார்த்தோம்!

The string of jasmine falls from Yashoda's hand.

**யசோதை** *(shocked)*
என்னது?! மண்ணா?!

Kannan strolls in casually behind them. Yashoda grabs his wrist. This time it's real worry, not play.

**யசோதை** *(scolding, worried)*
ஏன்டா இப்படிப் பண்ற? வயிறு வலிக்கும்னு எத்தனை தடவை சொல்றது!

Kannan doesn't hide his hands. He doesn't do the innocent eyes. He looks straight at her, calm, almost too calm. Lamp light flickers in his eyes.

**கண்ணன்** *(steady, slow, no giggle)*
அம்மா, நான் தின்னல. இவங்க எல்லாரும் பொய் சொல்றாங்க. நம்பலைன்னா... என் வாயைப் பாரு!

Yashoda kneels and holds his chin.

**யசோதை** *(firm)*
சரி... வாயைத் திற! காட்டு!

Kannan smiles a tiny, knowing smile. The boys lean in behind her. All sound drains away. Even the crickets stop.

---

## SCENE 8: வாய்க்குள் ஒரு பிரபஞ்சம் (THE UNIVERSE IN HIS MOUTH)

### INT./EXT. முற்றம் → THE COSMIC VISION – CONTINUOUS
**Background:** the courtyard fades as a golden-white light pours from Kannan's mouth. The vision is vast, reverent, and painted in gold, white and deep indigo. It is never frightening.

**Story beat:** the climax of the episode. Yashoda, and the audience, see who this child really is.

Kannan slowly opens his mouth. **Golden-white light** floods out and lights Yashoda's face from below. Her eyes go wide.

We fly *into* the light: into an endless starfield, spiral galaxies turning in silence.

**கதைசொல்லி** *(hushed, full of wonder)*
அந்தச் சின்ன வாய்க்குள்ள... யசோதா பார்த்தது மண் இல்ல...

The sun, the moon and the planets turn slowly in deep indigo space. Stars swirl.

**கதைசொல்லி**
...முழு பிரபஞ்சத்தையும்! வானம்... சூரியன்... சந்திரன்... நட்சத்திரங்கள்...

Majestic mountains, endless oceans and islands are seen from impossibly high above, with clouds drifting across them.

**கதைசொல்லி**
...மலைகள்... கடல்கள்... தீவுகள்... இந்த பூமி முழுசும்...

Wind swirls, sacred fire blazes, and lightning cracks across the clouds.

**கதைசொல்லி**
...காற்று... நெருப்பு... மின்னல்...

We dive down through the clouds to a tiny village on a shining river. It's Gokulam.

**கதைசொல்லி**
...அதுக்குள்ளேயே... கோகுலம்!

Down into a familiar courtyard, where a woman kneels before a glowing child. **It is Yashoda herself.**

**கதைசொல்லி** *(slow, the most important line)*
...அங்கே, கண்ணன் முன்னாடி மண்டியிட்டு உட்கார்ந்திருக்கிற... அவங்களையே!

Back to the real Yashoda. She trembles. A tear runs down her cheek. Stars are reflected in her eyes.

**யசோதை** *(whispering)*
இது... கனவா? இல்ல... மாயையா?

Her face slowly changes from fear to understanding to reverence.

**யசோதை** *(inner voice, echoing)*
இல்ல... இவன் சாதாரணக் குழந்தை இல்ல... இந்த உலகத்தையே தாங்குறவன்...

Slowly, Yashoda joins her palms in prayer before the radiant child. Behind her, the three boys bow too. The music rises to its peak.

## SCENE 9: மாயை (SHE FORGETS, AND LOVES)

### EXT. முற்றம் (COURTYARD) – DUSK, CONTINUOUS
**Background:** the cosmic glow fades back into the warm lamplight of the courtyard at dusk.

**Story beat:** the heart of the series' theme. Kannan doesn't want to be worshipped by his mother; he wants to be loved by her. He lets her forget. (From the Bhagavatam: he covers her with his maya in the form of a mother's love.)

Close on Kannan's eyes: gentle, loving. A soft golden mist begins to rise from him.

**கதைசொல்லி** *(tender)*
அப்போ கண்ணன் நினைச்சான்... அம்மா என்னைக் கடவுளாப் பார்க்க வேண்டாம். அம்மாவாவே பார்க்கட்டும்.

The mist drifts over Yashoda. Her folded hands loosen. The light of the vision fades from her eyes. She blinks, confused. In front of her is only her little boy, with a smudge of mud on his lip.

**யசோதை** *(puzzled smile)*
...என்ன யோசிச்சேன்? ம்ம்... ஞாபகமே இல்லையே...

She scoops him into her lap and kisses his forehead.

**யசோதை** *(loving)*
என் கண்ணா... இனிமே மண்ணு திங்கக் கூடாது, சரியா?

**கண்ணன்** *(cheeky again, giggling)*
சரிம்மா...

The camera rises above the lamplit courtyard: a mother rocking her child.

**கதைசொல்லி**
வேதங்கள் தேடுற இறைவனை... யசோதா தன் மடியில கொஞ்சிக்கிட்டு இருந்தாங்க.

Off to the side, Shridama and Subala look at each other, completely baffled.

**ஸ்ரீதாமா**
என்னடா நடந்துச்சு?

**சுபலன்** *(shrugging)*
தெரியல... ஆனா எனக்குப் பசிக்குது!

---

## SCENE 10: இரவு (NIGHT, AND THE HOOK)

### INT. படுக்கை அறை (BEDROOM) – NIGHT
**Background:** a small room. A wooden cradle hangs from the beam by ropes. A single clay oil lamp glows in a wall niche, and silver moonlight comes through a small window. The silhouette of the butter pot in its sling is visible above.

**Story beat:** a quiet ending that turns into the question of the series, "Who is he, and how did he come here?", then a hard cut to the threat that will drive Part 1.

Yashoda gently rocks the cradle. Kannan sleeps, one hand still curled around his wooden cow.

**யசோதை** *(lullaby, soft)*
ஆராரோ ஆரிரரோ... என் கண்ணே கண்ணுறங்கு...

Moonlight falls on his sleeping face. She looks at him for a long moment, as if trying to remember something she can't quite reach.

**யசோதை** *(whispering)*
நீ யாருடா கண்ணா...? யாரா இருந்தாலும்... நீ என் பிள்ளை தான்.

### EXT. கோகுலம் (GOKULAM) – NIGHT
**Background:** the whole village asleep under a full moon, the Yamuna shining silver.

**கதைசொல்லி**
ஆனா... இந்தக் குழந்தை கோகுலத்துக்கு எப்படி வந்தான்?

### EXT. மதுரா (MATHURA) – STORMY NIGHT
**Background:** sudden contrast: a dark stone fortress city on a hill, black clouds, rain, lightning. Grey, black and blood-red.

**SMASH CUT.** Thunder. Lightning tears across the sky over Mathura.

**கதைசொல்லி** *(darker)*
அந்தக் கதை... ஒரு புயல் இரவுல... ஒரு சிறைச்சாலையில ஆரம்பிச்சது.

### INT. மதுரா சிறை (MATHURA PRISON) – NIGHT
**Background:** a stone cell, iron bars, chains, a burning torch, puddles reflecting the lightning.

In a lightning flash: the silhouettes of a chained couple, **Vasudeva and Devaki**, holding each other. A guard slumps against the bars, suddenly asleep.

### INT. கம்சனின் அரண்மனை (KAMSA'S PALACE) – NIGHT

Darkness. A flash of lightning reveals only **a pair of burning eyes** beneath a jagged gold crown.

**கம்சன்** *(deep, cold, afraid)*
என்னைக் கொல்லப் போறவன்... எங்கேயோ பிறந்துட்டானா?!

Thunder. Black.

**END CARD** *(golden particles; a peacock feather; the flute motif)*
**அடுத்த அத்தியாயம்: சிறையில் ஒரு இரவு**

## After the screenplay: what's from tradition and what's ours
| From Srimad Bhagavatam 10.8 | Our creative additions |
|---|---|
| Butter and curd stolen from neighbours' houses with friends | Gomathi Akka, Paati, Kamala (names and personalities) |
| Climbing on mortars to reach hanging pots; making holes in pots | The human tower and the stick |
| His body lighting up dark rooms | Shridama's "விளக்கே வேண்டாம்" line |
| Feeding monkeys; breaking the pot if they won't eat | குரங்கு மாமா as a recurring character |
| Gopis complaining to Yashoda; Krishna looking innocent | The "I slept here all morning" trick and the wink |
| Balarama and the boys report the mud-eating; Krishna says "look in my mouth" | Combining all of this into one day |
| Yashoda sees the universe, Vraja and herself; wonders if it's a dream; surrenders | Yashoda's spoken lines in the vision |
| Krishna spreads his maya as maternal love; she forgets and takes him on her lap | The lullaby and the "நீ யாருடா கண்ணா?" scene |
| Kamsa learns his killer is born elsewhere (10.4) | Kamsa's line as a teaser |
