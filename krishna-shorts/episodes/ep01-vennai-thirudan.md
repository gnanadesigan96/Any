| | |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | Little Kannan and his friends build a tower to raid the butter pot Yashoda hung out of reach. ### Source & adaptation
| Traditional (Srimad Bhagavatam 10.8.29–39) | Creative adaptation in this episode |
|---|---|
| Krishna steals butter and curd from hanging pots, climbing on mortars and stools to reach them | All dialogue |
| He feeds the stolen butter to monkeys and to his friends | The friend-on-the-mortar "tower" staging |
| He talks his way out when caught | The specific "the monkey smeared it on me" excuse |
| Balarama and the boys complain to Yashoda that Krishna ate mud; she tells him to open his mouth | Compressing the butter theft and the mud complaint into the same morning |

1. Generate **one file per line** (13 lines + narrator ×2). Name them `EP01_S08_L1_yashoda_t1.wav`.

### E5. Mix levels (CapCut)
- Dialogue: peaks around **−6 dB**; it must always be the loudest element.
- Music under dialogue: **−22 to −26 dB**; between lines it can rise to −14 dB.
- SFX: −12 to −18 dB (the pot crash and drum hit can peak at −8 dB).
- Export loudness target: about **−14 LUFS** (YouTube/Instagram normalise to around this).

4. Add music cues, then SFX; apply the levels above (use keyframes or auto-ducking).
