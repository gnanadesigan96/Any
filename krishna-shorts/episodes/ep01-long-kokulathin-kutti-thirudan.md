## A. Story summary

| | |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | One day in Gokulam. Little Kannan raids a neighbour's butter with his friends, talks his way out of the gopis' complaints, raids his own mother's pot, and then eats mud on the Yamuna bank. When Yashoda makes him open his mouth, she sees the entire universe inside, including herself. Kannan lets her forget, so she can go on loving him as just her child. That night, as she wonders who he really is, the story flashes back to a stormy night in a prison… |
| **Arc** | Mischief → comedy → love → awe → tenderness → mystery |
| **Characters** | Child Krishna (Kannan), Yashoda, Balarama, Shridama, Subala, Gomathi Akka (neighbour gopi), 2 gopis, monkeys, Narrator; glimpses of Vasudeva, Devaki and Kamsa (silhouettes) |
| **Emotional objective** | The audience falls in love with Kannan as a child, feels Yashoda's love, is awestruck at the divine reveal, and wants to know where he came from |
| **Next episode** | அத்தியாயம் 2: சிறையில் ஒரு இரவு (the birth in Kamsa's prison, told as a flashback) |
